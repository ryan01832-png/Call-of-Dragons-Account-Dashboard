# Building schema cleanup rules

- Game master constants are separate from player account state.
- Buildings are displayed in player-friendly groups: Core / Command, Research & Development, Military / Training, Healing & Recovery, Support / Alliance, Resource Production.
- Celestial is troop/combat taxonomy and must not be modeled as a city building.
- Unknown values remain unknown; do not infer or fabricate CP curves.
- Rhino remains the seed player profile; selectors mutate account state only.
- Verified screenshot/master-repository values outrank external references.
